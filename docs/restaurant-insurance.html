Restaurant Insurance

Coverage for eligible restaurants, bars and food service operators. Built around the dining room, the kitchen, the bar and the business behind them.

Restaurants & Bars

Protect everything behind tonight’s service so you can keep serving tomorrow. A restaurant carries property, customer, alcohol and digital exposure at the same time, and each one is a different policy.

What the Policy Covers

Coverage
What It Covers
Small Business (BOP)
Combines common small-business property and liability protections into a single insurance package.
Commercial General Liability
Protects your restaurant against certain claims if a customer is injured or their property is damaged because of your operations. Typical limits $1M per occurrence and $2M aggregate.
Commercial Property
Protects your restaurant’s building, equipment, inventory, furniture, and other covered property from covered losses.
Liquor Liability
Protects restaurants that sell or serve alcohol against certain claims arising from intoxicated customers who cause injury or property damage.
Cyber Liability
Helps protect your restaurant against certain costs and liabilities resulting from cyberattacks, data breaches, and compromised information.
Umbrella Liability
Provides additional liability limits above qualifying underlying policies when a covered claim exceeds those limits.

FAQ

What liability limits are available?expand_more

Typical general liability limits are $1 million per occurrence and $2 million aggregate. Limits are subject to underwriting and higher limits may be available.

Does receiving a quote mean coverage has started?expand_more

No. A coverage discussion does not bind insurance. Coverage begins only when a policy is issued, subject to its terms, limits, deductibles and exclusions.

How are property limits set?expand_more

Property limits depend on insured values and the quote. Other coverage limits, deductibles and terms vary by policy.

Can I combine coverages?expand_more

Yes. A Business Owner’s Policy combines common small-business property and liability protections into a single package. Additional coverages can be added depending on your operations.

What determines eligibility?expand_more

Eligibility depends on your operations, location, condition and loss history. Being listed on this page is not a guarantee of acceptance.
